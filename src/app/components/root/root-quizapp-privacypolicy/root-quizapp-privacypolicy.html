ProfitPiller Quiz Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the ProfitPiller Quiz app for Android ("the app", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies only to the ProfitPiller Quiz app. Use of the profitpiller.com website is covered by our main Privacy Policy.

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. About the app

ProfitPiller Quiz is a free trivia game. You can play without creating an account, signing up or logging in, and the app has no in-app purchases or payments.

Coins, hearts, power-ups, badges and avatars exist only inside the game. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, gift cards, prizes or any other reward.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the app keeps the following on your phone or tablet only:

  • Game progress: coins, hearts, level and XP, scores and personal bests.
  • Daily features: daily challenge results, streaks, missions, lucky spin and daily reward status.
  • Collections: badges, power-ups and avatars you have unlocked.
  • Settings: sound on or off and whether reminders are turned on.

This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your Android settings, deletes it permanently.

3. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera. We do not sell personal information.

4. Advertising

The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Some ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example to double your coins or get an extra heart. To show, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID.
  • Approximate location based on your IP address.
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps.
  • Device and diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system version and crash reports.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your ad choices
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Settings › Google › Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can still play if you decline; you may then see non-personalised ads.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications and schedules one reminder per day, for example that a new daily challenge is ready. Reminders are created on your device; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app or in your Android notification settings at any time.

6. Sharing your score

When you tap "Challenge a friend" or "Invite", your phone's share sheet opens with a message containing your score and a link to the app. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send the message yourself. We do not see who you share with.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. We do not keep a copy. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own retention policies; you can manage it through your Google account and Android ad settings.

9. Security

Information sent between the app and Google's advertising services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the app does not store personal information on our servers, most requests can be handled on your device: clear the app's data to delete your progress, and use your Android ad settings to manage advertising. For anything else, contact us below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page. If the app starts handling information in a new way, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.
