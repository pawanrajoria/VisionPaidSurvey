ProfitPiller Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the ProfitPiller Puzzle app ("the app", "we") handles. The app is a free daily puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no payments.

Information the app stores on your phone

Your game progress is saved only on your device: coins, hints, stars, streaks, puzzle results, your chosen language and settings such as sound. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

On first start the app creates a random player ID on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number, and is used only for the optional surveys described below.

Language

To suggest a language, the app reads your phone's language and region settings and time zone on the device. This information is not sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about how you interact with ads, and
  • diagnostic data such as crash reports.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data in partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings under Google › Ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Optional surveys

The Surveys button opens the ProfitPiller Offerwall, operated by ProfitPiller. Using it is optional. When you open it, the offerwall receives your random player ID, your chosen language, your time zone, your IP address and the type of device you use. If you choose to answer its short profile questions (for example age, gender or postal code), those answers are stored by ProfitPiller so that you are only shown surveys you qualify for.

Surveys are run by market-research partners. When you start a survey, the partner receives the information needed to run it and your answers to that survey, under its own privacy policy shown at the start of the survey. Completed surveys earn in-game coins.

To have the survey data linked to your player ID deleted, contact us at the address below. Reinstalling the app also gives you a new player ID that is not linked to your earlier answers.

Leaderboard

To rank players, the app sends each solved daily puzzle (which game, the day, stars and time) to our server, together with your random player ID and your country code (taken from your phone's region setting). If you set up a leaderboard profile, your chosen display name, avatar and status are stored too and shown to other players. You don't have to set a name: without one you appear as "Player" with a number. Avatars and statuses come from a fixed list. To have your leaderboard data deleted, write to us at the address below.

Reporting and hiding players. You can report another player from their leaderboard profile (for example for an offensive name). The report stores your random player ID, the reported player's public ID and name at that time, the reason you chose and the date. Reports are not shown to other players or to the reported player. We use them to review names; a name reported by several players is replaced with "Player" automatically. You can also hide a player: the list of players you hide is kept only on your phone and is not sent to us. You can also report a name by writing to us at the address below.

Sharing your result

When you use "Share", your phone's share sheet opens with a message containing your stars and a link to the app. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The surveys are only for adults.

Virtual items

Coins, hints and streak freezes exist only inside the game. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, prizes or rewards.

Security

All data the app sends (to Google AdMob, the ProfitPiller Offerwall and our leaderboard server) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Deleting your data

See how to delete your ProfitPiller Puzzle data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
