Delete your ProfitPiller Puzzle data

App: ProfitPiller Puzzle (com.profitpiller.puzzle) · Developer: ProfitPiller · Last updated: 22 October 2026

ProfitPiller Puzzle has no accounts. Your game progress lives on your phone. Some data reaches our servers only when you use the leaderboard or the optional surveys. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@profitpiller.com with the subject "Delete my ProfitPiller Puzzle data".
  2. Tell us your leaderboard name and avatar (in the app: Leaderboard › Me), and the country you play from, so we can find your player record. If you never set a leaderboard name, say so and tell us roughly when you started playing and whether you used the surveys.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the data within 30 days.

On your phone: uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings › Apps › ProfitPiller Puzzle › Storage, immediately deletes everything stored on the phone (coins, progress, streaks, Puzzle Art, album, settings) and your random player ID. After that, nothing new can be linked to your old records.

What we keep, and for how long

DataKept
Records of completed surveys and the coins they paidKept up to 45 days after the survey, the window in which survey partners can reverse a completion. After that they are kept only in anonymous form, without your player ID, for accounting.
Reports other players made about your leaderboard nameKept in anonymous form for up to 90 days to prevent abuse, then deleted.
Server backupsDeleted data disappears from backups within 30 days.

Advertising data

Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which processes data such as your device's advertising ID under Google's own policy. We cannot delete Google's data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads, and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
